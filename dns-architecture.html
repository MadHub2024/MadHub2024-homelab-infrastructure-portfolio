Case study 02

Layered DNS Architecture

Building internal authoritative DNS, local service discovery, and network-wide filtering with clear separation of responsibilities.

Technitium DNSAdGuard HomeSplit DNSDoHTroubleshooting

Challenge

The network needed reliable internal names across multiple zones, filtering for all VLANs, and predictable troubleshooting. Ad hoc rewrites alone did not provide the authoritative structure required for a growing self-hosted environment.

Architecture

Clients
  ↓
Technitium DNS (primary LAN resolver)
  ↓
AdGuard Home (filtering)
  ↓
Encrypted upstream resolver

Technitium owns internal zones and service records. AdGuard Home applies filtering and forwards external requests to encrypted upstream resolvers.

Internal zones

The environment uses multiple local namespaces, including .home, .dbservices, and an internal view of dbservicessolutions.org. These zones provide consistent names for Caddy-published applications and direct infrastructure access.

Troubleshooting

  • Used dig against each resolver to isolate failures.
  • Validated authoritative zones and record ownership.
  • Investigated SERVFAIL responses and DNSSEC-related behavior.
  • Aligned host and container DNS behavior with the intended resolution path.

Result

The network now has centralized naming, filtering, and clear fault boundaries. Internal service discovery remains available without exposing private applications to public DNS.
